隐私政策

适用应用：颠球 · Bounce（iOS / watchOS）　·　生效日期：2026 年 11 月 25 日　·　最近更新：2026 年 11 月 25 日

一句话说明：颠球不收集你的个人信息。没有账号、没有广告、没有第三方统计 SDK，练习记录只保存在你的设备上，并仅在 iPhone 与 Apple Watch 之间直接传输。

1. 本政策的适用范围

本政策适用于 iOS 与 watchOS 应用「颠球 · Bounce」（以下称「本 App」），由个人开发者（以下称「我们」）提供。本 App 无需注册即可使用，我们不会要求你提供姓名、邮箱、手机号等身份信息。

2. 本 App 会处理哪些数据

为提供计数、记录与统计功能，本 App 会在你的设备上处理以下数据：

数据 用途 存放位置 是否上传
练习记录：球类项目、次数、时长、开始时间 显示首页进度、周 / 月统计与历史记录 你的 iPhone / Apple Watch 本机 否
偏好设置：每日目标、计时长度、提示音与轻触反馈开关、健康写入开关 记住你的使用习惯 你的 iPhone / Apple Watch 本机 否
手腕动作数据（设备运动传感器） 实时识别颠球动作并计数 仅在内存中实时处理，不保存原始传感器数据 否
体能训练记录（仅在「写入「健康」」开启后） 让你在「健康」App 中与其他训练一起回顾 由系统「健康」App 管理，保存在你的设备上 否

以上数据均不会离开你的设备，也不会传输给我们或任何第三方。本 App 没有服务器，不存在上传接口。

3. 我们不会收集的信息

  • 姓名、邮箱、电话号码、邮寄地址等身份信息；
  • 通讯录、照片、日历、麦克风、相机、精确位置；
  • 广告标识符（IDFA）、设备指纹等用于广告或追踪的标识；
  • 崩溃日志、使用行为统计、页面埋点等分析数据；
  • 任何账号、密码或支付信息（本 App 不含内购与订阅）。

本 App 不集成第三方广告、分析或社交 SDK，也不进行跨 App 或跨网站追踪。这一点与随 App 一同发布的隐私清单（PrivacyInfo.xcprivacy）一致：不进行追踪（NSPrivacyTracking = false），未声明收集任何数据类型。

4. 系统权限说明

权限 用途 是否必需 如何关闭
「健康」写入权限 把每次练习的项目、时长与次数保存为一条体能训练 可选，默认不开启 系统「设置」→「隐私与安全性」→「健康」→「颠球」；或在「健康」App → 头像 →「隐私与安全性」→「App」→「颠球」

本 App 只申请写入「健康」的权限，不会读取你的健康数据。关闭该权限后，计数、记录与统计功能不受影响，仅不再写入「健康」。手表自动计数使用设备运动传感器，不需要额外的系统授权提示。

5. 数据在设备之间如何传输

iPhone 与 Apple Watch 之间的练习记录同步，使用 Apple 提供的 WatchConnectivity 在设备之间直接完成。传输由系统加密，不经过我们或任何第三方的服务器；我们无法访问这些内容。

6. 数据保留与删除

  • 练习记录与设置会一直保留在你的设备上，直到你删除 App 或在系统设置中清除数据。
  • 卸载本 App 会一并删除本机上的全部练习记录与偏好设置，此操作不可恢复。
  • 已写入「健康」的体能训练由「健康」App 管理，需要你在「健康」App 中单独删除。
  • 如果设备开启了 iCloud 备份，本 App 的数据会随设备备份一起保存，直到该备份被覆盖或删除。我们没有能力访问或删除你的设备备份。

7. 儿童隐私

本 App 不会收集任何个人信息，也未针对 13 岁以下儿童设计定向内容或广告。家长或监护人如果对儿童使用本 App 有疑问，可以通过下方邮箱联系我们。

8. 数据安全

由于数据只保存在设备本地，其安全由 iOS / watchOS 的沙盒机制与设备加密保护。请妥善保管设备与设备密码；如果你的设备与他人共用，建议开启系统的锁屏密码与数据保护。

9. 第三方服务

本 App 仅使用 Apple 提供的系统框架（HealthKit、WatchConnectivity）实现「写入健康」与设备同步。这些功能由 Apple 提供并受 Apple 的隐私政策约束，你可以随时在系统设置中关闭相关权限。

10. 政策变更

如果本 App 的功能发生变化（例如未来增加云同步或分析功能），我们会更新本页内容并修改上方的「最近更新」日期；涉及重大变化时，会在 App 内或本页显著位置提示。继续使用本 App 即表示你接受更新后的政策。

11. 联系我们

对本政策或数据处理方式有疑问，欢迎发邮件至 839055164@qq.com，我们通常会在 1–2 个工作日内回复。

Privacy Policy

App: Bounce (iOS / watchOS)　·　Effective date: November 25, 2026　·　Last updated: November 25, 2026

In one sentence: Bounce does not collect your personal information. There is no account, no advertising and no third-party analytics SDK. Your practice records stay on your devices and are transferred only directly between your iPhone and Apple Watch.

1. Scope

This policy applies to the iOS and watchOS app “Bounce” (the “App”), provided by an individual developer (“we”, “us”). The App works without registration, and we never ask for your name, email address or phone number.

2. Data the App processes

To provide counting, logging and statistics, the App processes the following data on your devices:

Data Purpose Stored on Uploaded
Practice records: sport, count, duration, start time Show today's progress, weekly / monthly statistics and history Your iPhone / Apple Watch No
Preferences: daily goals, timer length, sound and haptic toggles, Health write toggle Remember how you like the app to behave Your iPhone / Apple Watch No
Wrist motion data (device motion sensors) Detect bounces and count them in real time Processed in memory only; raw sensor data is never stored No
Workouts (only when “Write to Health” is enabled) Let you review practice sessions alongside other workouts in the Health app Managed by the system Health app on your device No

None of this data leaves your devices. The App has no backend servers and no upload endpoint.

3. What we never collect

  • Identity information such as your name, email address, phone number or postal address;
  • Contacts, photos, calendar, microphone, camera or precise location;
  • Advertising identifiers (IDFA), device fingerprints or any data used for advertising or tracking;
  • Crash reports, usage analytics or behavioural event logs;
  • Accounts, passwords or payment information (the App has no in-app purchases or subscriptions).

The App contains no third-party advertising, analytics or social SDKs and performs no cross-app or cross-site tracking. This matches the privacy manifest shipped with the App (PrivacyInfo.xcprivacy): tracking is disabled (NSPrivacyTracking = false) and no data types are declared as collected.

4. System permissions

Permission Purpose Required How to revoke
Health write access Save each session's sport, duration and count as a workout Optional, off by default iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Health → Bounce, or the Health app → your profile → Privacy & Security → Apps → Bounce

The App requests write access only and never reads your health data. Revoking it does not affect counting, logging or statistics — sessions simply stop being saved to Health. Automatic counting on Apple Watch uses the device motion sensors and requires no additional permission prompt.

5. Device-to-device transfer

Records sync from Apple Watch to iPhone using Apple's WatchConnectivity framework, directly between your devices. The transfer is encrypted by the system and never passes through our servers or any third party. We cannot access it.

6. Retention and deletion

  • Records and settings remain on your device until you delete the App or erase your device.
  • Deleting the App removes all local records and preferences. This cannot be undone.
  • Workouts already written to Health are managed by the Health app and must be deleted there.
  • If iCloud Backup is enabled, App data is included in your device backup until that backup is overwritten or deleted. We cannot access or delete your device backups.

7. Children's privacy

The App collects no personal information and contains no content or advertising targeted at children under 13. Parents or guardians with questions are welcome to contact us at the address below.

8. Security

Because data stays on your device, it is protected by the iOS / watchOS app sandbox and device encryption. Please keep your device passcode safe, especially if the device is shared with others.

9. Third-party services

The App only uses Apple system frameworks (HealthKit and WatchConnectivity) for Health integration and device sync. These features are provided by Apple and governed by Apple's privacy policy; you can revoke the related permissions in system settings at any time.

10. Changes to this policy

If the App's features change (for example, if cloud sync or analytics were ever added), we will update this page and the “Last updated” date above, and we will highlight material changes in the App or at the top of this page. Continued use of the App means you accept the updated policy.

11. Contact

Questions about this policy or how the App handles data? Email 839055164@qq.com and we will usually reply within 1–2 business days.
